NOVATECH REMOTE WORK FAQ & GUIDELINES

1. REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees with at least 30 days of tenure are eligible for hybrid or remote work arrangements, subject to manager approval.

2. HOME OFFICE SETUP ALLOWANCE
New remote employees are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of $500 to purchase ergonomic desk equipment, monitors, or keyboards.

3. CORE WORKING HOURS
Remote team members must remain accessible on Slack and email during core working hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their respective time zone.

4. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Home internet connection is subsidized at $40 per month for remote staff.
